# Legal and publication checklist

This repository's original documentation is Apache-2.0 licensed. That does not grant rights to the service's trademarks or confidential user data.

Before accepting production users:
- [ ] Identify actual data controller/legal entity and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Review applicable privacy notice and records of personal-data processing, retention and deletion.
- [ ] Confirm cookie and tracking claims against real website behaviour.
- [ ] Separate access-request communications from optional marketing subscriptions.
- [ ] Implement a reliable unsubscribe and suppression process before marketing messages.
- [ ] Publish enforceable customer service terms, acceptable-use rules and support/security intake.
- [ ] Define scope and liability for officially announced vs predicted holiday dates.
- [ ] Inventory redistributable font and third-party licenses; retain notices.
- [ ] Map proprietary product marks separately from Apache-2.0 sources.
- [ ] Validate production release ID, commit SHA, approval, operational rollback and incident ownership.

These are approval requirements, not a statement that legal review has already passed.
